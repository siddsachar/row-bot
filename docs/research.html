Row-Bot Research

How personal agents learn.

Studies of local-first AI agents: how an agent improves from the work it does, what it should remember, and what runs well on your own computer. Each study is fixed in advance, and every result is reported.

How we work

The rules every Row-Bot Research study follows. They hold whether or not a result favours Row-Bot.

  • Fixed in advance

    Confirmatory studies are written down before they run, and their SHA-256 hash is made public. Any later change is a dated amendment, never an edit.

  • Every result reported

    Null and negative results are reported in full, and so are runs that could not be made. Nothing is left out because it makes Row-Bot look worse.

  • Reproducible

    Each paper names the exact code commits, scripts and hashes behind its numbers, and publishes the aggregate reports.

  • No personal data

    Studies use seeded, simulated people and public tasks. A study on one person's real work publishes aggregates only, never their conversations or files.

  • Interests declared

    The researcher builds Row-Bot. Every paper that studies Row-Bot says so in a competing-interests statement.

  • AI assistance disclosed

    Code, analysis and drafts are written with AI coding agents under the researcher's direction. No AI system is listed as an author; the human author is responsible for every claim. Errors are corrected with a dated note.
