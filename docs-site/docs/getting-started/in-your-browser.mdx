# Row-Bot In Your Browser

A hosting service can run your own private Row-Bot for you on a small server. There's nothing to install: you open it from any browser, on a computer or a phone, and it keeps working while your devices are off, so schedules and channels keep running.

It's the same Row-Bot as the desktop app, and it's yours alone: one owner, signed in from as many devices as you like. You bring the model: a ChatGPT or Claude plan, an API key, or your own model server.

Your host runs the server; Row-Bot runs on it. [What Your Host Can See](/docs/privacy-safety/what-your-host-can-see) says plainly what that means for your data.

## Start

- **On Railway:** **Continue with Railway** at [row-bot.ai/railway](https://row-bot.ai/railway/) creates your Row-Bot in your own Railway account in about 3 minutes and opens it signed in. [Row-Bot On Railway](/docs/getting-started/on-railway) has the steps, the template you can deploy by hand instead, and what it costs.
- **On another host:** any service that runs Row-Bot's container image can host it. [Hosting Row-Bot](/docs/operations/hosting) has what the image needs.

## Enter The Setup Code

The setup code makes your browser the owner, so nobody else can use your Row-Bot. **Continue with Railway** uses it for you and opens Row-Bot signed in. Otherwise:

1. Find the code. On Railway, it's the sign-in link in the service's deploy log, or `ROW_BOT_SETUP_CODE` in its **Variables** ([Sign In](/docs/getting-started/on-railway#sign-in)). On another host, it's on the line that starts `Row-Bot setup code:` in the server's log.
2. Open your Row-Bot at its address. It says **Welcome to your Row-Bot**.
3. Paste the code into **Setup code** and choose **Continue**. Dashes, spaces and capitals don't matter.

Row-Bot says "This browser is signed in." and asks how it should think. The code works once: from then on, anyone else who opens your address sees **This Row-Bot already has an owner**, and you add your other devices from Row-Bot itself ([Add Your Phone](#add-your-phone)).

Keep the code private until you've used it. If you can't find it, or later lose access, see [Lost The Setup Code](/docs/troubleshooting/hosted#lost-the-setup-code).

## Choose A Model

Row-Bot asks **How should Row-Bot think?** with two big buttons:

- **Sign in with ChatGPT**: one click copies a code and opens OpenAI's page in a new tab. Paste the code there and approve; Row-Bot notices when you're done.
- **Use my Claude plan**: Row-Bot installs Claude Code, Anthropic's program, on your server, then opens Claude's own sign-in in a small panel. Open the link it shows, sign in to Claude, and paste the code Claude gives you into the panel. Your plan's limits apply, and Row-Bot never sees, stores or relays your Claude sign-in.

**Other ways** holds **Use an API key** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter and more), **Use my own model server** and **Sign in with Grok**.

After you sign in, Row-Bot picks that provider's first model, checks that it answers, and opens a chat with one suggestion to tap: **What can you do for me?** Change the model any time in the model picker. [First Launch](/docs/getting-started/first-launch#on-a-hosted-row-bot) explains each choice.

Your server has no graphics card, so it can't run chat models itself. To use models on hardware of your own, connect it as [your own model server](/docs/guides/own-model-server), or use the desktop app.

## Add Your Phone

Opening your Row-Bot's address on another device isn't enough: Row-Bot doesn't know that browser yet. Sign the new device in from one that's already signed in:

- **After your first answer,** a card in that chat offers **Add Row-Bot to your phone**. On a computer, choose **Show the code** and scan it with your phone's camera. The phone signs in once and stays signed in.
- **Any time later,** open **Settings › Devices & remote access** and choose **Connect a phone or computer**. **Copy link** gives the same one-time sign-in as a link, for a device without a camera.

Then add Row-Bot to your phone's home screen, so it opens like an app:

- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, tap ⋮, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

**Settings › Devices & remote access** also lists every signed-in device, and signs one out.

## What's Different

A hosted Row-Bot is the full Row-Bot, without the parts that need your own computer or a graphics card.

### Works The Same

- Chat, agents, workflows and schedules, always on: they keep running while your devices are off.
- Memory, knowledge and documents, with Row-Bot's own search model.
- Row-Bot's browser: watch it work, take control, and sign in to a site once, from your phone too.
- Commands and Python, Developer Studio and Designer Studio.
- Apps, MCP servers, and channels such as Telegram and Slack.
- Row-Bot on your phone's home screen.
- ChatGPT sign-in and API keys.

### Different On A Hosted Row-Bot

- **Your files live on the server,** not on your computer. Attach a file in a chat to give it to Row-Bot.
- **Row-Bot says "your Row-Bot server"** where the desktop app says "this computer".
- **Websites see a datacenter's address** when Row-Bot's browser visits them, so some may ask for extra checks.
- **Your host installs updates.** On Railway, new versions install each night ([Updates](/docs/getting-started/on-railway#updates)). **Settings › Updates** shows your version and a **What's new** link.
- **Local speech-to-text needs a 4 GB server.** On a smaller one, **Settings › Voice** marks it **Needs a 4 GB server**: use cloud speech-to-text there.
- **Your Claude plan** works through Claude Code, which Row-Bot installs on your server when you choose **Use my Claude plan**.
- **Models on your own hardware** work through a custom endpoint your server can reach over the internet, with a key or a tunnel in front: see [Use Your Own Model Server](/docs/guides/own-model-server).
- **Keep a backup of your own.** Not every host backs up the server (Railway does only on its Pro plan): download a backup from **Settings › Data** from time to time ([Moving To Your Computer And Back](#moving-to-your-computer-and-back)).

### Not On A Hosted Row-Bot

- Chat models running on the server itself, since it has no graphics card. Your own model server elsewhere works.
- Computer use of your own screen, and your own browser with its sign-ins. Row-Bot's own browser works.
- The desktop window, your computer's file pickers, and a terminal window on your computer.

For those, use the [desktop app](/docs/getting-started/installation). You can move between the two whenever you like.

## Costs

- **The server:** billed by your host for what it uses ([Railway's costs](/docs/getting-started/on-railway#costs)). Row-Bot needs 4 GB for local speech-to-text; 2 GB also works if you use cloud speech-to-text.
- **The model:** billed by whoever provides it. A ChatGPT or Claude plan is the subscription you already pay for, with its limits. An API key is billed by its provider for what you use. Your own model server costs whatever running it costs you.
- **Row-Bot itself** is free and open source, and bills you for nothing.

## Moving To Your Computer And Back

Your Row-Bot isn't tied to its host. A backup carries your conversations, memories, workflows, designs and settings between a hosted Row-Bot and the desktop app, either way.

To move to your computer:

1. On your hosted Row-Bot, open **Settings › Data** and choose **Download backup**.
2. [Install Row-Bot](/docs/getting-started/installation) on your computer. At first launch you can choose **Set up later**.
3. Open **Settings › Data**, choose **Restore from backup…**, pick the file, and choose **Restore on restart**. The restore applies the next time Row-Bot starts.
4. Sign in to your model again, and set up whatever the backup lists under "After restoring it, set these up again".

To move back, choose **Back up now** in the desktop app's **Settings › Data**. Then, on your hosted Row-Bot, choose **Restore from backup…** in **Settings › Data**, pick the file, choose **Restore on restart**, and restart Row-Bot on your host (on Railway, redeploy the service from its **Deployments** tab).

A backup never holds passwords, API keys or sign-ins, your Claude plan's included, so you sign in again after moving. It also leaves out Row-Bot's browser sessions, your signed-in devices, and downloaded models and runtimes, which you download again where you need them. Once everything has arrived, you can remove the old server from your host.

## Getting Help

- **Your host** helps with the server: billing, its size, its backups, and whether it's up. In Row-Bot, **Settings › System** shows who hosts it, with links to manage the server and to the host's support. On Railway, see [Getting Help](/docs/getting-started/on-railway#getting-help).
- **Row-Bot** helps with everything inside Row-Bot: chats, models and sign-ins, workflows, memory, apps, the setup code and the out-of-memory notice. Start with [Fix A Hosted Row-Bot](/docs/troubleshooting/hosted). Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/siddsachar/row-bot/discussions), or [report a bug](https://github.com/siddsachar/row-bot/issues/new/choose): under "Where does Row-Bot run?" choose a hosting service, and name your host and your server's size. Monitor's **Copy diagnosis report** gives a summary to paste in.
