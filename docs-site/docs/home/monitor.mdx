# Home: Monitor

The Monitor tab answers: is everything healthy, what needs attention, and what happened recently? Row-Bot keeps the latest result of every check, so the tiles stay current after you switch tabs or restart.

<Screenshot id="home-monitor" alt="Row-Bot Home Monitor tab with health tiles, a problem and its fix." caption="Monitor lists what needs attention with its fix, then health tiles, activity, maintenance, history and logs." />

## What You Can Do Here

- **Needs attention** lists problems with a one-click fix, such as **Restart** a stopped channel or **Reconnect** an account.
- **Health tiles** show each area's latest checks; open one for every check and its fix.
- **Run diagnosis** runs every check now; **Copy diagnosis report** copies a summary for support.
- **Check connections every hour** controls the checks that contact your providers, accounts and the internet.
- **Activity**, **Maintenance**, **History** and **Logs** show what ran and why.
