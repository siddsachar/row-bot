# Home: Overview

Overview is the first page Row-Bot opens. It is one calm column that says the most important thing first: what needs you, then where you left off, then what is happening today. It shows only what exists, so a quiet day is a short page. Everything on it opens the place where the work actually lives.

## Where To Find It

Choose **Home** in the sidebar. Overview is the first of the five Home tabs: Overview, Workflows, Knowledge, Monitor and Insights. Until a default model exists, opening Row-Bot opens [first launch](/docs/getting-started/first-launch) instead; if you choose **Set up later**, Overview shows only **Choose a model** under the greeting.

## The Greeting And The Ask Box

- One sentence under the greeting says the most important thing: how many things need you, else when the next workflow runs, else **All quiet**.
- **Ask Row-Bot anything, or describe what to make…** starts a new chat with your text. Use **Add files in a new chat** to attach files first, then **Start chat**.
- Three small icons under the Ask box are quick starts (hover or focus one to see its name):
  - **New design** and **New code folder** set up a design or a code folder and open it in a new chat with its panel beside it. See [Designs](/docs/designer/) and [Code Folders](/docs/developer/).
  - **New workflow** opens the workflow editor on the Workflows tab.

## Needs You

**Needs you** appears only when something waits for you, with the same count as the sidebar's indicator:

- **Approvals** from any conversation, workflow or delegated agent show **Approve** and **Deny** right in the row, with how long each has waited. Open the conversation or workflow when you want the full context first.
- **Posts and emails to review**: one row, such as **2 posts and 1 email to review**, that takes you to **Review**.
- **Conversations waiting on you**: interrupted agent work offers **Resume** or **Dismiss**; a goal waiting for your answer opens its conversation.
- **Failed workflows** offer **Run … again**, or open the workflow to see why.
- **Apps that need you**: an app that is on but needs a sign-in, its key or attention shows as **Sign in to _app_**, **_App_ needs its key** or **_App_ needs attention**. Its fix opens the app in [Settings › Apps](/docs/settings/apps) with that fix started.
- **Problems** such as a stopped channel or a red health check carry their one-click fix, such as restarting the channel, reconnecting an account, choosing a model, or checking again. See [Monitor](/docs/monitor/).
- **Finish setup** shows as the last row while Setup Center still has steps. It is a reminder, not a need, so it isn't counted; hide it once you are done.

Three items show at first; **+N more waiting** shows the rest.

## Review

**Review** shows each post or email waiting to go out as its full card: the account or recipients, the whole text, each picture or file, and, for a post with a time, when it goes out. Each card has **Approve** (**Approve for then** for a post with a time), **Deny**, the pencil to edit a post, and **Not now**, which sets it aside here while it keeps waiting. It works the same on a phone.

A post approved for a time is listed under **Scheduled** until it goes out. Choose **Cancel this post** to stop it. Row-Bot posts it at its time, while it is running. A post whose time passed more than 12 hours ago while Row-Bot was closed, or whose file changed after you approved it, isn't sent: you get a notice instead.

## Recent

**Recent** lists your last three chats, workflow conversations or code folders, newest first, each with when you last used it. **All** opens the [Conversation library](/docs/chat/#find-export-and-clean-up).

## Today

**Today** shows up to four short rows, and only the ones that exist:

| Row | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| An agent at work | Monitor |
| When the next workflow runs | That workflow on the Workflows tab |
| A health warning, with its fix beside it | Monitor |
| Memories saved this week | Knowledge |

A warning Monitor kept from an earlier check, such as a channel you have since turned off, isn't shown until the checks run again. If a source can't be read, Today says so in one quiet line.

With [Proactive Row-Bot](/docs/guides/proactive) on, Today becomes **For you**, placed after Review: what Row-Bot brought up, grouped by **Now**, **Later today** and **Ready when you are**, starting with [your morning brief](/docs/guides/morning-brief) and ending with **That's everything for today.** Today's rows move into it. With Proactive **Off**, Today stays as described here.

The rest of what used to sit here lives in its tab: agent and workflow activity in [Monitor](/docs/monitor/) and [Workflows](/docs/home/workflows), memories in [Knowledge](/docs/knowledge/), and insights in [Home: Insights](/docs/home/insights).

## What Is Saved

Overview stores nothing of its own. It reads your conversations, workflows, approvals, memories and Monitor checks, and every action runs through the same reviewed command as the place it comes from.

## Troubleshooting

- If Overview opens Setup instead, finish choosing a model or choose **Set up later**.
- If Today says it couldn't read something, refresh the page; Monitor shows whether the service behind it is healthy.
- Approving from Overview is the same decision as approving in the conversation. Open the conversation first when the request is not obvious from its title.
