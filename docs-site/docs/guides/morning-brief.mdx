# Your Morning Brief

The morning brief is your day in a few lines: today's meetings, replies you're owed or owe, dates coming up, and what Row-Bot prepared overnight. It ends, every time, with **That's everything for today.**

It is part of [Proactive Row-Bot](/docs/guides/proactive) and is on at every level except **Off**.

## When It Appears

The first time you open Row-Bot or come back to your computer each day, after 06:00 and after your quiet hours:

- **Buddy** says it in one line, such as "Good morning. 3 meetings, 2 follow-ups, and Dad's birthday is Sunday.", with **Open brief**.
- **Home › For you** shows it first, opened, until you close it or the afternoon starts. After midday it folds to its one line at the end of For you.

It is never sent to your phone or a chat app. It waits for you.

## What It Covers

```
Good morning. 3 meetings, 2 follow-ups, a birthday.
10:00  Design review with Priya and Tom · brief ready
13:00  Lunch with Sam
15:30  1:1 with Ana (moved from 14:00)
Priya hasn't replied about Q3 (4 days) · draft ready
Tom asked about the venue · draft ready
Dad's birthday is Sunday
Overnight: Row-Bot drafted 2 replies and a brief.
That's everything for today.
```

- **Meetings** for today, with a brief where one is ready and any change since yesterday.
- **Follow-ups**: replies you're waiting for and ones waiting on you, with drafts where Row-Bot wrote one.
- **Dates** coming up: birthdays, renewals, trips and deadlines.
- **Overnight**: what Row-Bot prepared while you were away, such as meeting briefs and drafts. This line shows only when there is something.

Each line opens its own thing: the brief, the draft, the event. Things in the brief don't also show as separate rows that day, unless they become urgent; a meeting brief still appears ten minutes before the meeting.

On a quiet day the brief is short ("Good morning. A quiet day: one meeting at 15:00."). With nothing on, Buddy says nothing and For you shows only "Good morning. Nothing on today."

The brief only reads. Nothing is sent or changed; sending a draft always goes through its approval card.

## Change It

The brief is a workflow you can edit, like any other:

- **⋯ › Edit brief** on the brief opens it in the workflow editor. Change its message to cover what you want, such as "Also list my top three tasks from the Tracker".
- **⋯ › Open conversation** opens the brief's own conversation, where each day's brief is a turn. You can reply there to ask about it.
- **⋯ › Less like this** and **⋯ › Why this?** work as they do for every moment.

See [Workflows](/docs/guides/workflows) for the editor.

## Turn It Off

Choose **⋯ › Turn off the morning brief**, or turn off **Morning brief** in **Settings › Proactive › What to bring up**. Proactive keeps working without it; the things it would have listed still wait on Home.

## The End-Of-Day Wrap-Up

The wrap-up is the brief's evening partner, off until you turn it on in **Settings › Proactive › What to bring up**. At the time you choose (17:30 to start with), Buddy says something like "Wrap-up: 4 things done, 1 moved to tomorrow." and For you shows:

- what got done today;
- what moved to tomorrow;
- what is still waiting on someone else.

It ends with **That's everything for today.** Like the brief, it is an editable workflow and never goes to your phone or a chat app.

## Troubleshooting

- **No brief this morning.** Check that Proactive isn't **Off** and **Morning brief** is on. It comes at your first open after 06:00 and after quiet hours; a day with nothing on has no bubble.
- **The brief misses meetings or mail.** It uses the calendars and mail accounts you've connected and switched on under **Sources** in Settings › Proactive.
- **It came at the wrong time.** It comes at your first open after your quiet hours: set **Quiet hours** in Settings › Proactive.
