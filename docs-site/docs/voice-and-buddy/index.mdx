# Voice And Buddy

Voice and Buddy make Row-Bot feel less like a text box and more like a desktop companion. Voice handles speech input and optional spoken output. Buddy is a living character that reacts to what Row-Bot is doing and, in the native Windows and macOS apps, can be dragged from the sidebar onto the desktop as a pet that sits on your windows.

## Voice Modes

- **Dictate** turns speech into composer text so you can review it before sending.
- **Talk** is a spoken conversation with Row-Bot Voice: hands-free, with the chat's own model, tools, memory and approvals.
- **Read aloud**, a button on each reply, speaks that reply in the voice chosen in Settings › Voice: Kokoro on the computer running Row-Bot, or a cloud voice you chose. Talk's spoken answers use the same voice.
- **Local voice** uses Whisper by default, or the optional SenseVoice Small engine after you install it explicitly. For Talk, a **faster speech model** picked for your computer (Moonshine Base on a processor or a Mac, Nemotron Speech Streaming on an NVIDIA graphics card the speech runtime can use) shows your words as you speak and answers sooner; download it under Settings › Voice › Talk.
- **Live voice** (optional) talks through a cloud speech-to-speech model: OpenAI Realtime, Gemini Live or Grok Voice, with your own key for that provider. It knows the chat's recent messages, the agent profile's name and a few relevant memories, answers small talk itself and hands real work to Row-Bot, which uses the chat's own model, tools and approvals. While Row-Bot works it tells you what it is doing, and it says Row-Bot's answer when it is ready. Its own replies are saved in the chat, marked as Live voice lines.

## Start Talking

In a chat, the **Talk** button (beside the microphone that dictates) opens its menu: **Talk with Row-Bot** starts Row-Bot Voice, and the **Live voice** rows start a cloud provider (see [Live Voice](#live-voice)). On a phone with no Live voice key saved, one tap starts Row-Bot Voice. Talk waits while a reply is still running. Home's Ask box starts voice in a new chat too (see [Home And Phones](#home-and-phones)).

Row-Bot hears when you start and stop speaking. It answers when you pause; **When you pause** in Settings › Voice sets how soon. Talk over Row-Bot and its voice drops at once; keep talking and it stops, and the rest of that answer stays on screen. A short "mhm" or "okay" doesn't stop it. After five minutes with nothing said, voice pauses; tap it to resume.

## The Voice Bar

Voice runs in a bar above the composer. Its round button shows the state (**Listening**, **Thinking**, **Speaking**, **Waiting for you**, **Muted** or **Paused**): tap it to stop Row-Bot speaking, to unmute, or to resume. The bar's buttons, each with its name as a tooltip:

- **Hold to talk**, with push to talk on (see [Push To Talk](#push-to-talk)).
- **Share camera or screen** (see [Show Row-Bot Your Camera Or Screen](#show-row-bot-your-camera-or-screen)).
- **Microphone and speaker** (the headphones button) picks the devices (see [Microphone And Speakers](#microphone-and-speakers)).
- **Show captions** or **Hide captions**: your words and Row-Bot's, as they are said. **Live captions** in Settings › Voice sets the default.
- **Mute** or **Unmute**.
- **Type instead** mutes and shows the composer; press it again to go back to talking.
- **Show details**: how quickly Row-Bot replied and the language it last heard.
- **Full screen**, on a phone.
- **End voice**.

The bar also says which resources spoken requests can change, what to do when an action needs your OK, and when a key, a device or a language is missing.

## Live Voice

Open the **Talk** menu in a chat and choose a Live voice provider. Each one shows its approximate price a minute (from the provider's published prices, with the date checked); the provider bills your key, not Row-Bot. A provider without a saved key says so and links to Providers. Before you use a provider the first time in a browser, a panel says plainly that your voice, the chat's recent messages, the agent profile's name and a few related memories go to that provider while you talk (in an incognito chat, no memories), and you choose **Start** with the provider's name to go on. Live voice then runs in the same voice bar as Row-Bot Voice, with its captions and controls. It is always hands-free: push to talk is for Row-Bot Voice.

Your provider key never leaves the computer running Row-Bot. For Gemini Live and Grok Voice, the page connects only to Row-Bot, which relays your voice and the provider's speech. For OpenAI Realtime, Row-Bot sets up the call with a short-lived credential it keeps, and the call's audio then goes directly between your browser and OpenAI. Row-Bot Voice, on your own computer, stays the default.

## Show Row-Bot Your Camera Or Screen

While you talk, the video button in the voice bar shares your camera or, in a desktop browser or the Windows app, a screen or window. On a phone it shares the camera, and **Switch camera** flips between front and back. Nothing is captured until you choose to share. While sharing, the bar always says **Camera on** or **Sharing your screen** with a small live preview (larger in the phone's full-screen view), and one tap on the button stops it. Ending voice, or a long quiet pause, stops sharing too. Sharing goes on while you are muted.

With Row-Bot Voice, nothing is streamed: when you finish saying something, Row-Bot takes one still of what you are showing and sends it with what you said, as an image in your message. You see exactly what was sent, and it is kept like any image you attach (in an incognito chat too, with the chat's other files). If the chat's model can't see images, the bar says so and links to the Vision model setting; nothing is sent. Something you say while Row-Bot is still working steers it with your words only.

Gemini Live sees what you share as well: it is shown a still about every two seconds while you share, so it can talk about it, and its disclosure says so. OpenAI Realtime and Grok Voice don't take pictures in a live session, so they offer no sharing.

## Wake Word

Turn on **Hey Row-Bot** in Settings › Voice, under **Wake word**, to start talking without a tap. It is there only on the computer running Row-Bot (in the Row-Bot app, or a browser on that computer), never on a phone or another device. While a Row-Bot window is open, the window listens for "Hey Row-Bot" and the computer running Row-Bot recognises it with a small model that ships with Row-Bot. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. When it hears the phrase, Row-Bot Voice starts in the open chat, or in a new chat if none is open.

While it listens, the navigation shows **Listening for "Hey Row-Bot"**. Click it to stop listening in that window, and click again to start again. While voice is on, the wake word waits and its microphone is closed. Closing the window stops it. If your computer stops the microphone while the window is hidden, the mark says so and listening resumes when the window is back. "Hey robot" sounds the same, so Row-Bot wakes to that too.

## Choosing A Mode

Use Dictate when accuracy and review matter. Use Talk when you want a conversation. Use local voice when privacy and offline-style behavior matter. Whisper remains the default local transcription engine; a faster speech model makes Talk quicker. SenseVoice is an optional alternative on Windows, Linux, and Apple Silicon macOS; Intel macOS continues to use Whisper. A cloud speech service (**Listen with**) or a cloud voice (**Speak with**) in Settings › Voice keeps Row-Bot Voice but uses that company, with your key. Use Live voice for the most natural conversation when you accept that audio goes to the provider you choose, at its price.

## Languages

Row-Bot hears which language you speak (**Language** in Settings › Voice, **Detect automatically** by default), and you can choose one instead. Whisper understands every language in the list; the faster speech models understand English only, and Settings says so if you speak another. Kokoro answers in English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. It can't speak other languages, such as Japanese or Chinese, yet: those answers stay on screen and the voice bar says so. A cloud voice speaks the languages its company lists. See [Settings: Voice](/docs/settings/voice).

## Voice Notes In Chat Apps

On Telegram and WhatsApp, a voice note you send is transcribed on the computer running Row-Bot, and Row-Bot answers with its text and then a voice note, in the voice chosen in Settings › Voice. Turn this off with the channel's **Reply to voice notes with a voice note**. See [Channels](/docs/integrations/channels).

## While Row-Bot Works

When you talk with Row-Bot Voice and it uses a tool, it says a short line such as "Checking your calendar…" so you know it is working, and "Still working on it…" if nothing has been said for a couple of seconds. These lines are spoken only: they are not saved in the chat and the model never sees them. They never play over you or in the middle of an answer.

You can keep talking while it works. Ask "how's it going?" and it tells you what it is doing without stopping. Anything else you say goes to the work in progress, and Row-Bot says "Got it, I'll do that next." When helpers finish after the turn has ended, their answer is spoken at the next pause, as long as voice is still on. Pictures and videos appear in the chat as they are made; in the phone's full-screen voice view, the newest one shows under the captions and opens with one tap.

When something needs your OK, Row-Bot says what it is and the approval card appears. For an action that can be undone, such as moving a file or adding a calendar event with no guests, you can say "yes" or "no" once it has finished asking ("Say yes or no, or tap the card"). Anything that deletes, sends, posts, pays, publishes, runs a command or uses an app's tool needs a tap on the card, and Row-Bot says "This one needs a tap on the screen." A "yes" said over Row-Bot's own voice, a mixed answer such as "yes, but…", or silence never approves anything.

## Optional SenseVoice Setup

Open Settings -> Voice, find **SenseVoice** under **Voice models**, and choose **Install SenseVoice Small**. Then choose it under **Speech engine** (**Talk listens with** or **Dictation listens with**). Installation is an explicit approximately 940 MB download from ModelScope. Row-Bot verifies the contained snapshot before saving it under the active data directory, and normal transcription then uses that local snapshot with update checks disabled. Startup, readiness checks, and ordinary transcription never download or repair the model automatically.

## Microphone And Speakers

Row-Bot records and plays voice in your browser. Choose the microphone (and, in Chrome, Edge and the Windows app, the speaker) under **Settings › Voice › Microphone and speaker**, or from the headphones button in the voice bar while you talk. Devices have the names your operating system gives them, a Bluetooth headset included; the browser shows the names once Row-Bot may use the microphone. The choice is kept in that browser only. Safari, iPhone and Firefox play through the system's own output, so they offer no speaker choice.

If the microphone you chose is disconnected while you talk, Row-Bot keeps listening with the default one at once and the bar says so; when it is connected again, Row-Bot goes back to it. The same goes for the speaker.

## Push To Talk

Hands-free is the default: Row-Bot hears when you start and stop. Turn on **Push to talk** under Settings › Voice › Talk to have it listen only while you hold **Hold to talk** in the voice bar, or hold **Space** while you aren't typing in Row-Bot's window. In the Windows app you can also choose a key to hold anywhere, even while another app is in front (**Hold a key to talk**: Ctrl + Alt + Space, Ctrl + Shift + Space or F13). It is off until you choose one, works only while voice is open with push to talk, and Row-Bot listens for that one key only; while it is on, other apps don't get that key. On a Mac this isn't offered yet.

## Home And Phones

Talk and Dictate start from Home's Ask box on a computer too: with nothing typed, its microphone dictates and its Talk button talks in a new chat.

On a phone, Row-Bot keeps the screen on while you talk. A phone stops listening when the screen locks or turns off, or when you switch to another app: on iPhone that includes a Row-Bot added to the Home Screen, and on Android the screen turning off. The voice bar says so once. Where the phone or browser shows media controls (the lock screen, a headset's button), they show **Row-Bot · Listening**; play and pause unmute and mute, and hanging up ends voice.

## Buddy Controls

- **Show Buddy** controls companion visibility.
- **Drag to tear off** moves Buddy itself from its sidebar dock onto the desktop, where it lands on a window's top edge or above the taskbar or Dock. Releasing over the dock cancels the drag.
- Click the desktop Buddy to open its speech bubble. The bubble follows the selected conversation, whether it is a chat, a design or a code folder, and shares its saved draft, current response, tools, and approval mode.
- Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line. **Stop** stops the selected thread's active response.
- **Simple approvals** can be approved or denied in Buddy. **Complex approvals** open the full thread for review.
- **Open full thread**, **Dock Buddy**, and **Hide Buddy** are in the bubble and in Buddy's right-click menu.
- Tear-off is session-scoped; restarting Row-Bot returns placement to the normal sidebar dock. Browser/server mode does not create a desktop pet.
- **Personality** changes the tone of Buddy cues, and **Bubbles** changes how status appears.
- **Look** chooses a bundled or custom appearance; **New look…** makes one with your image model.
- **Motion** chooses how lively Buddy is: **Calm**, **Normal** or **Lively**.

See [Settings: Buddy](/docs/settings/buddy) for the complete drag, messaging, approval, tray-recovery, and troubleshooting walkthrough.

## Talk To Buddy

Buddy's bubble accepts typed messages for the thread named at its top. It does not contain a separate microphone control: Talk and Dictate remain in the full Row-Bot thread, where device state, transcription, and provider disclosure are visible. Use **Open full thread**, start Talk or Dictate there, and Buddy will continue reflecting that selected conversation.

While you talk, Buddy shows that it is listening and its mouth moves with Row-Bot's voice, with Row-Bot Voice, Live voice and Read aloud alike, in the sidebar and as the desktop pet. Undocking Buddy does not change which voice provider is used, create a new voice session, or bypass tool approvals.

## Privacy And Safety

What you say becomes chat text, and goes to the chat's model like anything you type. Beyond that, each part of voice stays on the computer running Row-Bot unless you choose a company for it:

- **Row-Bot Voice** (the default): your browser sends your voice only to Row-Bot, over its own signed-in connection. Whisper, SenseVoice or a faster speech model writes it down there, and Kokoro speaks there. Nothing goes to a speech service.
- **A cloud speech service** under **Listen with** (Deepgram, AssemblyAI, OpenAI or Google): the stretches where you speak go to that company, one at a time, while you talk. Dictation and chat-app voice notes stay local.
- **A cloud voice** under **Speak with** (ElevenLabs, Cartesia or Google): the text of each sentence Row-Bot says goes to that company, for Talk, Read aloud and chat-app voice notes. **Describe a voice** sends only your description, never a recording.
- **Live voice** (OpenAI, Google or xAI): your voice, the chat's recent messages, the agent profile's name and a few related memories (none in an incognito chat) go to that provider while you talk, and so do Row-Bot's answers and short progress lines, which it says for Row-Bot; Gemini Live is also shown what you share on camera or screen.
- **Camera or screen**: captured only after you start sharing. With Row-Bot Voice, one still per thing you say leaves the page, saved in your message like an attached image, and goes where your images go (the chat's model or its Vision model).
- **Wake word** (if you turn it on): the Row-Bot window on the computer running Row-Bot listens for "Hey Row-Bot", and that computer recognises it. Nothing it hears is kept or sent anywhere.
- **Downloads**: speech models download only when you press **Download** or **Install**: Whisper from Hugging Face, Kokoro and the faster speech models from GitHub, SenseVoice from ModelScope. Installing SenseVoice sends no audio, prompts or usage data; inference stays local after installation.
- **Devices and controls**: the microphone and speaker you choose are kept in your browser and never sent to Row-Bot. The system's media controls show only **Row-Bot · Listening** (or what voice is doing).

Every cloud key is yours; the company bills it, not Row-Bot. Buddy preferences and looks are local and Buddy animates on your computer; making a new look calls your configured image model. The desktop Buddy reads only the position and size of the window you are using (never its title or contents) to sit on it.

## Troubleshooting

- If Dictate records nothing, check that the browser may use the microphone, and which microphone is chosen in Settings › Voice › Microphone and speaker.
- If a Bluetooth headset sounds muffled while you talk, that is the headset switching to its call mode (a lower sound quality) to use its microphone; choose the computer's own microphone to keep the headset's full sound.
- If Read aloud says it needs the Kokoro voice, install Kokoro under **Read aloud** in Settings › Voice.
- If Talk answers before you have finished, set **When you pause** to **Give me time to think** in Settings › Voice, or turn on **Push to talk**.
- If Talk doesn't hear you on a phone, check that the screen is on and Row-Bot is the app in front: phones stop listening otherwise.
- If SenseVoice reports missing packages, install the `voice` extra for a source checkout or repair the packaged app. If its snapshot is missing or incomplete, reinstall it from Voice settings.
- If SenseVoice is unavailable on Intel macOS, select local Whisper instead.
- If a Live voice provider is unavailable, add that provider's API key in Providers.
- If Buddy does not appear, enable **Show Buddy** or use the tray's **Show Buddy** action when it is torn off.
- If Buddy will not undock, use the native Windows or macOS app and start the drag on Buddy itself. Remote browsers and browser/server mode keep it docked.
