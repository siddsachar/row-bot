# Row-Bot Documentation

Row-Bot is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer. It chats, remembers what matters to you, works with your documents and apps, browses the web and uses the apps on your computer while you watch, runs workflows on a schedule, and makes designs and code. Your data stays on your machine, and you choose which models it uses, from a free local model to the ChatGPT, Claude or Grok plan you already have. Use it on your desktop, from your phone, or as a private server.

These pages describe Row-Bot 5.3.2.
