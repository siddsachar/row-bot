# Ask Row-Bot About Row-Bot

Row-Bot can answer questions about itself in the chat: how to do something, where a setting is, what it is running now, and what needs your attention. It answers from its own guides, which come with every install, so it works offline and matches the version you have.

## How Do I…?

Ask in your own words, such as "how do I turn off install stats?" or "where do I change the Dream Cycle hours?". Row-Bot searches its guides and answers in steps:

- Each setting it names is a chip, such as **Preferences › Dream Cycle**. Select it to open that row in Settings, highlighted, just as **Find a setting** does.
- The guide it used is a chip too. Select it to read that guide in a sheet beside the chat (from the bottom on a phone), opened at the section it cited. **Open on row-bot.ai** opens the same page on the web.
- If the guides don't cover something, Row-Bot says so instead of guessing.

## Settings Row-Bot Can Change

For some settings Row-Bot offers to make the change for you. It checks the value first, then asks on a card that shows what the setting is now and what it becomes, with **Show in Settings** to see the row. The chat's approval level decides who approves, as for any action:

- **Ask**: you choose **Change** (or **Turn on**), or **Not now**.
- **Auto**: the reviewer approves a change you clearly asked for and asks you about anything else. Turning on **Shell**, file tools, **Browser**, **Computer use** or **MCP** apps always asks you.
- **Hands-off**: it makes the change and records it.
- **Block**: it changes nothing.

Row-Bot can change your models (main, vision, image and video), the reading limit, its name and personality, **Learn new skills**, the Dream Cycle and its hours, **Read aloud**, **Live captions**, **Log detail**, which skills are available or pinned, and which tools are on.

After a change, its card in the chat offers **Undo**. Undo puts the old value back at once, with nothing to approve. If the setting has changed again since, Undo leaves it alone and says so. You can also ask Row-Bot to change it back.

Some settings stay yours alone. Row-Bot shows where they are but never changes them: approval levels, keys and sign-ins, devices and remote access, install stats, Learning, access to Row-Bot's data folder, updates, the workspace folder, appearance, anything that downloads or installs, and anything that deletes.

## What's Wrong?

Ask "what's wrong?" or "why didn't that work?". Row-Bot reads the same checks as [Monitor](/docs/monitor/) and Overview's **Needs you**, most urgent first, each with its one fix:

- It checks again when you ask. That contacts your providers and accounts, as Monitor's **Run diagnosis** does.
- Fixes only you can do, such as signing in again or restarting a channel, come back as a link to the exact place.
- Row-Bot never asks for a key and never signs in for you.

## Status

Type **/status** in the composer for a short summary: the version and any update, the model and whether it can use tools, this chat's approval level, and what needs you, each with its fix.

## In Chat Apps

In Telegram, Slack and other chat apps, Row-Bot writes settings as words, such as "Dream Cycle (Settings › Preferences)", and guides as their page on row-bot.ai.
