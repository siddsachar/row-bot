# Settings: Accounts

Settings no longer has an Accounts page. Your GitHub, Google and X accounts are apps now: open **Settings › Apps** and choose **GitHub**, **Google** or **X**. On the app's page the account is a **Built in** way to connect, and **Set up** (or **Open** once it is set up) opens that one account's own settings: its setup guide, **Check**, **Reconnect** and **Disconnect**, and what Row-Bot may do with it.

Read [Settings: Apps](/docs/settings/apps#built-in-ways-to-connect) and [Accounts And Authorisation](/docs/integrations/accounts).

Old Accounts links open the matching app in Row-Bot.
