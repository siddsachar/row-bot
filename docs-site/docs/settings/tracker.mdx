# Settings: Tracker

Tracking keeps structured personal logs, such as habits, symptoms, moods or health events, that you ask Row-Bot to record in a chat ("I drank 8 glasses of water", "Log a headache, 6 out of 10"). Row-Bot keeps each kind of record as a tracker.

<Screenshot id="settings-tracker" alt="Settings: Tracker in Row-Bot." caption="The Tracker page lists your trackers; open one to see its latest entries." />

## Where To Find It

Open **Settings**, then choose **Tracker** in the **Knowledge** group, or search for it with **Find a setting**.

## Controls

- **Track in chat** turns tracking on or off. While it is off, Row-Bot doesn't record or read trackers.
- **Trackers** lists each tracker with its kind and how many entries it has. Choose a tracker's name to see its latest entries (the newest 50, with the total); nothing there changes an entry. Its delete button deletes that one tracker and its entries, after you confirm.
- **Start a tracker in chat** opens a new chat with "Start tracking " ready in the message box, so you can say what to track. Trackers are added by asking Row-Bot in a chat.
- **Danger zone › Delete all trackers** deletes every tracker and entry, after you confirm.

## Common Workflow

1. Choose **Start a tracker in chat** and say what you want to track.
2. Keep logging in any chat ("Log 30 minutes of running").
3. Open the tracker here to check its latest entries, or ask Row-Bot to summarise them.
4. Delete a tracker you no longer use.

## What Is Saved

Tracker data is local Row-Bot data. It can influence answers only when Row-Bot is allowed to retrieve or summarize it.

## Privacy And Safety

Review credential, account, channel, provider, or tool settings before enabling features that can contact outside services. Local-only features stay on your machine until you ask Row-Bot to use a provider, account, channel, MCP server, plugin, or tool that sends data elsewhere.

## Troubleshooting

- If a tracker is missing, check that **Track in chat** is on, then ask Row-Bot in a chat to start it.
- If a summary seems wrong, open the tracker here and check its latest entries.
- If privacy matters, do not include tracker details in prompts sent to hosted providers.
