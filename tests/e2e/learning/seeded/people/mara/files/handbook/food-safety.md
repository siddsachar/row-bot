# Food safety (approved)

Lantern Lane Community Kitchen. Approved by the kitchen lead, January 2026.

- Fridges run at 5 °C or below. Check and log the temperature at the start of every shift.
- Freezers run at -18 °C or below.
- Cooked leftovers are labelled with the date and kept for at most 2 days.
- Allergen labels go on every tray that leaves the kitchen. The 14 major allergens are listed on the wall chart.
- Hot food is held at 63 °C or above when it is served.
- Probe thermometers are wiped with a sanitiser wipe between uses.
