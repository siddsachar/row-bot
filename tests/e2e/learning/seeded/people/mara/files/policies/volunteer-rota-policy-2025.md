# Volunteer rota policy (version 2)

Lantern Lane Community Kitchen. Approved by the trustees, September 2025.
**Superseded by version 3 (March 2026).** Kept for reference only.

## Volunteers per shift

| Shift | Volunteers |
|---|---|
| Weekday lunch (11:00-14:30) | 4 |
| Weekday dinner (16:30-20:00) | 5 |
| Saturday or Sunday (10:00-15:00) | 6 |

## Rules

- Minimum age to volunteer on a cooking shift: 16.
- Shift swaps: tell the coordinator at least 24 hours before the shift.
- A shift lead must hold a Level 2 food hygiene certificate.
- No limit on shifts per week.
