# Volunteer rota policy (version 3)

Lantern Lane Community Kitchen. Approved by the trustees on 12 March 2026. This is the current policy and
replaces version 2.

## Volunteers per shift

| Shift | Volunteers |
|---|---|
| Weekday lunch (11:00-14:30) | 5 |
| Weekday dinner (16:30-20:00) | 6 |
| Saturday or Sunday (10:00-15:00) | 7 |

## Rules

- Minimum age to volunteer on a cooking shift: 17. Volunteers aged 16 may help front of house.
- Shift swaps: tell the coordinator at least 48 hours before the shift.
- A shift lead must hold a Level 2 food hygiene certificate.
- At most 3 shifts per volunteer per week, so nobody burns out.
