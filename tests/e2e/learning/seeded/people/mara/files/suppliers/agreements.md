# Supplier agreements

## Hartwell Produce (fruit and vegetables)

- Delivers on Tuesdays and Fridays before 09:00.
- Minimum order: GBP 120.
- Orders close at 15:00 the day before delivery.

## Sandgate Bakery (bread)

- Delivers every day except Sunday.
- Minimum order: GBP 40.
- Price per large loaf: GBP 2.05 from 1 October 2026 (it was GBP 1.80 before that).

## Coldharbour Dairy (milk, butter, cheese)

- Delivers on Mondays and Thursdays.
- Minimum order: GBP 60.
- Returns of unopened stock are accepted within 3 days.
